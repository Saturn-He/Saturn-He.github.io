<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

I am **Jingfei He**, a Ph.D. student at [Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT)](https://www.bit.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. Liang Chen. In 2026, I was a visiting student at the [University of Pavia](https://www.unipv.eu/), Italy, with Prof. Paolo Gamba.

My research focuses on generative models for remote sensing, in particular **SAR-to-optical image translation**:

- **<span class="ri ri--1">Optical priors</span>**: guiding SAR-to-optical translation with representations learned from optical imagery.
- **<span class="ri ri--2">Controllable translation</span>**: controlling the colours of translated images with sparse colour anchors.
- **<span class="ri ri--3">Full-scene consistency</span>**: translating large scenes tile by tile while keeping the whole scene spatially consistent.

研究方向为遥感生成模型，主要关注 **SAR 到光学影像转换**：

- **<span class="ri ri--1">光学先验</span>**：利用从光学影像中学到的表征引导 SAR 到光学转换。
- **<span class="ri ri--2">可控转换</span>**：用稀疏颜色锚点控制转换结果的颜色。
- **<span class="ri ri--3">整景一致性</span>**：逐块转换大范围场景，同时保持整景的空间一致。
